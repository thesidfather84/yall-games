Endless Bay Privacy Policy

Updated November 9, 2026 · Android game version 1.0.0

Endless Bay is an offline turtle-cleaning game developed by Y’all Games, an independent studio in Louisiana, United States. Contact us at yallgames1984@gmail.com.

Information in the game

The game stores your score, personal best, number of turtles cleaned, simulated computer-rival scores and sound, volume and vibration preferences locally on your device. The rivals are computer-generated, not other online players. The game does not require an account or ask for your name, email address or other personal details. It has no advertising, analytics, tracking or in-app purchase service.

The game does not automatically send your saves or personal information to Y’all Games. Game artwork and audio are bundled for offline play. Its Android internet permission supports the local WebView address used to load the bundled game; it is not used for an advertising or analytics service. Optional vibration uses the device’s vibration service.

Optional developer diagnostics

Developer Mode is disabled by default. If you deliberately unlock it, test sessions use separate local progress, and a bounded log records the most recent gameplay events, numeric game state, error categories, app and Android versions, and display dimensions. Reports do not contain names, contact details, account information, device identifiers, authentication data, raw error messages or browsing history. There is no automatic upload or diagnostic server. Export Bug Report opens Android’s share sheet so you choose whether and where to send the report. Copy Bug Report, or the copy fallback when sharing is unavailable, places it on the device clipboard. Other applications or clipboard services you choose may handle the copied or shared report under their own policies. If you send a report to Y’all Games, we use it to diagnose and fix the problem, subject to the support-correspondence practices below.

Retention and deletion

Local progress and settings remain on the device until you clear the app’s storage, uninstall it, or the device removes them. Developer Options lets you reset test progress without changing normal saves, or clear the local diagnostic log. Disabling Developer Mode also clears that log. Use Android Settings → Apps → Endless Bay → Storage → Clear storage to delete all local data. Clearing storage starts the game afresh. Android’s cloud-backup setting is disabled for this app, and Y’all Games does not provide a cloud-save or recovery service. Some device manufacturers may still offer device-to-device transfer through Android system tools. We cannot recover or remotely delete saves that exist only on your device.

Support correspondence

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and the information or attachments you send. We use that correspondence to answer your request, resolve problems and meet applicable legal obligations. Please avoid sending sensitive information. Support email is handled through our email provider, Google. We retain correspondence only as needed for these purposes; contact the support address to request deletion. We do not sell support information or use it for targeted advertising.

Children and families

The game does not ask children for personal information or provide accounts, chat, advertisements or public user-content uploads. A parent or guardian should contact us on a child’s behalf and handle any optional diagnostic export. If we learn that a child has sent personal information unnecessarily, we will take steps to delete it, subject to any applicable legal requirement.

Google Play and external links

Google Play handles purchases and store activity under Google’s own policies. Y’all Games does not receive your payment-card information through the game. Opening an email or website link uses the corresponding external service. The studio website and this policy page are hosted by Vercel; the hosting provider may process basic request information, such as IP address and browser information, to deliver and secure the pages. Website requests are separate from your offline game saves.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if the game’s data practices change. The date above identifies the current policy. For privacy questions or support-data requests, email yallgames1984@gmail.com.
